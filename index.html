What this tool does

It externalizes differential reasoning by separating supportive findings, conflicting findings, and unresolved high-value questions.

What it does not do

It does not calculate likelihoods, make diagnoses, recommend treatment, or replace syndrome-specific diagnostic criteria.

Clinical caution

Comorbidity is common. A finding can support more than one diagnosis, and absence of a finding is not automatically treated as evidence against a diagnosis.
