# Evidence framework

Evidence reviewed November 2026.

The matrices summarize discriminating clinical features rather than reproducing proprietary diagnostic criteria. The app avoids numerical probability estimates and does not infer that absence of a feature automatically argues against a diagnosis unless an explicit, clinically meaningful absence relationship is encoded.
